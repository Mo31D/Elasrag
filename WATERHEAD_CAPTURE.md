# Photographs needed for Waterhead and Ambleside

- **Lakeside Picnic** · A level, straight-on photograph including the complete frontage and one oblique view from each pavement direction. Include the party walls or adjacent doors so the frontage can be matched to its exact building. Add a close-up of the signage and a tape measurement across the entrance or one window bay.
- **The Black Sheep Shop** · The complete ground-floor elevation and upper storeys, the approach from each side of the street, signage and door detail, and one frontage dimension. Record the entrance pin and capture date.
- **Waterhead Inn and Waterhead Coffee Shop** · Photograph the complete street-facing elevations, the ends of each roof section, each entrance and the junction between the Inn and its terrace. For the Inn include the white bay windows, gables, steps and recessed frontage.
- **Pier and promenade** · Retake the booking office from the opposite pier and from The Esplanade. Add the landward elevation, deck transition and railings, with approximate camera coordinates and date.

Use original photographs you have permission to share for modelling. Keep a level camera and include fixed architectural edges. Record the owner or photographer and permission for each set.
