Customers & professionals

Ready to grow

What you are reviewing today

Before the final version

What I need from BM HAIR.

The old website is useful as a reference, but it should not be the final source of truth. BM HAIR now needs to confirm the products and business information that are actually current.

Products

  • Which products are still sold
  • Which products should be removed
  • Any new products not on the old website
  • SKU for all products and variants

Product information

  • Final prices
  • Lengths, shades, textures and weights
  • Which images belong to which product / shade
  • Wig, hair-piece and custom-order details

Store information

  • Shipping countries and current delivery methods
  • Payment methods
  • Returns and guarantee policy
  • Final company / legal details

Why rebuild

Why the current website needs more than a visual update.

The current website contains valuable product knowledge, but its structure and technical setup make it harder to build trust, organise products clearly and grow into a modern multilingual online store.

Current website

Useful information, but a limited platform.

  • Security / HTTPS configuration needs attention before a modern launch.
  • Product and information content is difficult to organise clearly for search and customers.
  • The shopping journey is less clear than a modern e-commerce experience.
  • Multilingual growth and professional customers are not strongly separated.
New direction

A clear sales and brand platform.

  • Dedicated product and collection pages.
  • Clearer structure for Google and other search engines.
  • Modern mobile-first shopping experience.
  • Separate customer, custom-made and professional journeys.

Not just a website containing products — a complete BM HAIR digital platform.
