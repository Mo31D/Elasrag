WHY CHANGE THE CURRENT SITE

The problem is not only the design.

The technical review found issues that affect trust, search visibility and the shopping experience. The rebuild addresses these at the same time as the visual redesign.

Trust & security

The current secure-connection setup needs attention. Visitors should not see browser warnings or uncertainty before shopping.

Search visibility

Important products and content need dedicated, search-friendly pages and stronger technical search structure.

Shopping experience

The new structure makes products easier to browse, compare and eventually buy through a modern checkout.
