Why rebuild

The current website needs more than new colours.

The existing site contains valuable product knowledge, but its structure and technology make it harder to build trust, rank product content clearly in search, and scale the business into a modern multilingual store.

Current experience

Useful information, but difficult to scale.

  • Legacy page structure makes product discovery and SEO less clear.
  • Security / HTTPS configuration can create trust concerns for visitors and should be corrected for launch.
  • Products, articles, policies and company information are mixed into a dense experience.
  • International growth, multilingual SEO and professional customers are not clearly separated.
New direction

A clear website and commerce system.

  • Dedicated product, collection and guide pages that search engines can understand.
  • Modern responsive experience designed around trust and mobile shopping.
  • Separate B2C, custom-made and professional journeys.
  • Multilingual structure prepared for Latvia and wider European growth.
Important: the existing website was used as a starting source for product and business information. It should not be treated as the final catalogue until BM HAIR confirms what is still current.

Before final launch

What I need from BM HAIR.

The final catalogue should be based on current business data, not assumptions from the old website. BM HAIR's confirmation is the next important step before products, shipping and checkout are finalised.

Product catalogue

Confirm what is current

  • SKU for every product / variant
  • Current products and discontinued products
  • New products not shown on the old website
  • Final prices, sizes, lengths, shades and weights
Images & product details

Match each product correctly

  • Final product photography
  • Which image belongs to each colour / variant
  • Wig and hair-piece specifications
  • Confirmation of custom-order pricing
Store rules

Confirm how the business sells

  • Shipping countries, rates and delivery methods
  • Returns and guarantee policy
  • Payment methods
  • Final company / legal information
