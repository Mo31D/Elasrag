# Geographic acceptance standard

Report centreline offsets in metres with sample count, median, p95 and maximum. Use relative length error against independent measured route geometry. Compare footprint intersection-over-union, centroid offsets and orientation only against suitable independent footprints. Report the reference CRS, acquisition date and expected accuracy for each comparison. The target is approximately 1 m centreline deviation where supported, at most 1% route length difference and at least 99% surveyed footprint overlap. Include separate grades for connectivity, render continuity, collider alignment and actual traversal.

Current results measure source consistency and legacy coverage. They do not independently establish absolute road or building position. Complete the acceptance exercise with an independent dataset and on-device play-through before designating the world as verified.
