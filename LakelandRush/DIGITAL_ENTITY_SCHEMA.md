# Digital entities

Business IDs identify organisations. Premises IDs identify stable occupiable locations. Each premise points at its mapped building and location via provider-specific references. This permits multiple businesses in one building and changing occupancy without changing building geometry. The pilot records include The Black Sheep Shop and Lakeside Picnic. Verification currently describes owner identification, mapped premises and source lineage. It does not represent verified merchant accounts.

Future entities include entrances, interiors, landmarks, events, activities, offers, jobs and products. Publish owner-controlled commercial attributes separately from geographic source facts. Changes will carry evidence, authorship, validity dates and audit history.
