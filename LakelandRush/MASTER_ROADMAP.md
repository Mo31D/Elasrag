# Master roadmap

1. Geographic acceptance in Ambleside and Waterhead. Compare independently licensed OS geometry and LiDAR, inspect mesh and collision alignment, traverse both approaches and the shop-to-shop connection, and agree corrections with the founder.
2. Waterhead production art. Use matching reference cameras for a small waterfront benchmark. Then prove a portable character and animation pipeline.
3. Ambleside open world. Prioritise navigable streets, recognisable buildings, camera and mobile controls, and individually authored landmark interiors.
4. Controlled social and multiplayer. Introduce consent, privacy, moderation and explicitly fictional demos before connecting real users.
5. Holiday companion. Add local discovery, activities, accessibility, passports and private memories.
6. Business pilot. Validate premises and commercial interfaces for The Black Sheep Shop and Lakeside Picnic with owner-authorised sources.
7. Verified business management, community events and employment.
8. Optional GPS, with coarse social proximity considered separately.
9. Sandbox commerce and non-monetary rewards. Real financial activation requires separate approvals.
10. Extend the validated world to other settlements and regional services.

Phase 1 remains open. Do not promote the V2 preview over the V1 site until the founder accepts geographic and visual evidence.
