# Sign placement V8

The user observed a Climbing Wall bus-stop sign in the middle of Lake Road and inconsistent sign directions. The previous bus board used the source stop coordinates directly, while route signs used arbitrary segment order and could face either side. V8 preserves all source stop coordinates and searchable names but generates separate physical pole anchors. Bus signs are placed on the left of the bearing-aligned road, with bounded along-road and outward search, a whole-board clearance envelope and building/wall/water rejection. No valid candidate means no physical sign; the map record remains. The Climbing Wall stop is southbound (https://bustimes.org/stops/090079414625). Other bearings remain source/provisional. Street-name plates run parallel to road; route-name signs face approaching traffic on the left, and the fork board uses the incoming direction and same-side clearance. Generic invented welcome boards are disabled. The renderer and sign planner share road half widths; near tile edges the conservative guard suppresses poles. Sign styles are illustrative, not certified traffic sign surveys; exact curb, pole, shelter and street-name positions require field references. Existing business labels and facade signs retain their independent system. No roads or geographic source positions changed. Tests include actual Climbing Wall map data and both travel directions.
