# V16 wingsuit and facade signs
Free-flight mode now uses an articulated horizontal wingsuit pose, two deployable teal/gold arm membranes and a leg membrane attached to the original character. Ground stance, walking, jumps and flight controls are unchanged; the suit retracts upon exiting flight. No new asset downloads. This is a stylized game wingsuit, not a physics simulation or bird avatar. Floating dark business-name sprites removed; mapped facade signs are pale, borderless, flush to the wall/fascia, and authored landmark signage (including Lakeside Picnic) retained. Road and bus signs remain independent and double-sided. Unresolved businesses remain searchable; not all names yet have mapped facade positions. Mobile visual verification required.
