# V12 grass and parking
Terrain UVs now use metres rather than a single regional texture stretch. Procedural 512px meadow albedo and bump show blades, thatch and soil, with less saturated terrain tint. Nearby grass tufts use seven low-poly tapered blades and deterministic placement, one instanced mesh per visible nearby chunk, within 25 m of camera, regenerated after 5 m movement. Exclude known roads, buildings, water, parking and Waterhead mapped shore/sidewalk/aprons; unmapped vegetation remains approximate. Parking uses unmarked asphalt texture, tessellated terrain-following surfaces and estimated bays based on existing parking slots. Some bays remain empty. Existing OSM polygons and terrain unchanged. Exact paving and paint layout not surveyed. Actual mobile performance/appearance not yet tested.
