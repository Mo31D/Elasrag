# V14 — mobile scroll, vegetation and free flight
Native title/menu scrolling restored: zoom suppression applies only during active gameplay; no global CSS touch-action:none or global viewport zoom restriction. OS/browser overrides may still apply. Title content scrolls as a single panel.
Vegetation is now sparse, 16m near field, maximum 600 candidates per active chunk versus 4800 previously; GPU allocation lazy, no small-plant shadows, updates every 8m, hidden at aerial altitude. Four representative procedural types: short meadow, rough grass, autumn bracken and sparse muted flower heads. No actual botanical survey or exact current flowering claims. Existing mapped exclusions and parking retained.
Single jump height doubles (approximately 1.2m above takeoff on level ground); second press targets approximately 2.4m. Free exploration bird toggle enables flight; same joystick and pace, hold arrows for altitude, keyboard F/Space/Q. Altitude clearance 1.25m and limit 180m above local surface; toggle off restores falling/landing. Route restart resets flight; not available in indoor or race modes. Actual iOS scrolling, performance, flight/landing and visual vegetation quality need device testing.
