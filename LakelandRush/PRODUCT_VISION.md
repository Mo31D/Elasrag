# Product vision

Offer guest exploration and map-based discovery for visitors, then add games, virtual passport stamps and private holiday memories. The product should remain useful before, during and after a visit. Later, consenting people can join moderated activities and verified businesses can publish practical information. Accessibility requires usable lists and search alongside 3D. See [founder's vision](FOUNDERS_VISION.md) and [phase gates](MASTER_ROADMAP.md).
