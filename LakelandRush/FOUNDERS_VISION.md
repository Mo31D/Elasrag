# Lakeland World

**Explore. Play. Connect. Experience.**

Build a living, playable digital twin of the Lake District around authentic geography, original architecture and its real communities. Start with Ambleside and Waterhead, then expand across the region after the first town passes geographic and visual acceptance. Lakeland Rush remains an optional activity. Free exploration remains available to guests.

Visitors should be able to plan a trip, explore during a holiday and revisit private memories afterward. Residents should find genuinely useful local discovery, activities and entertainment. Verified businesses should manage their own digital presence and eventually connect it to services, events, jobs, shopping, fulfilment and independently approved rewards.

Real people, merchants, physical visits, fictional profiles and simulated transactions must remain distinguishable. Social, multiplayer, adult dating and proximity discovery require separate consent and safeguards. Commerce and financially valuable rewards require explicit approval and appropriate review.
