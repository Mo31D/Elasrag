# V11 — exploration controls
Walk 6 mph; Faster walk 10 mph; Run 20 mph. Values are gameplay speeds at full joystick deflection, not ordinary human walking speeds. Existing internal walk/run/sprint keys retained. Indoor and arcade race speeds unchanged. Free exploration permits a second distinct jump press before landing; the ballistic target is twice the single-jump height above takeoff on unobstructed level ground. Third presses ignored until landing; reset on route change, and no keyboard-release jump cut in exploration. Browser zoom is suppressed with existing viewport meta, CSS touch-action and non-passive double-tap/pinch/gesture handlers. Browser and OS accessibility overrides may still apply. Real iOS device verification remains required.
