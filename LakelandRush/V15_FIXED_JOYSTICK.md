# V15 fixed joystick
The previous floating joystick intercepted the entire lower-left 46% × 43% of the viewport and relocated its centre on every touch. The new movement control is a stationary 110px disc, anchored at the safe-area bottom-left. Only touches beginning within its visible circle start movement; pointer capture tracks that finger until release, including outside the circle. Independent canvas pointer capture handles camera rotation with a second finger. Camera gestures never move the joystick centre. Stale camera capture is released on menus, blur and visibility changes. V14 title scrolling, zoom restrictions, flight, jump and movement speeds retained. Pure tests cover hit radius, centre stability and movement saturation; actual simultaneous iOS touches still need device testing.
